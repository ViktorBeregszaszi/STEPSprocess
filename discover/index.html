What is STEPS Discover

A physical workshop for mapping operational reality

STEPS Discover uses cards, role lanes, and prompts to help a team co-create a visual map of a real procedure or workflow.

The result is not a theoretical process diagram. It is a shared working map, built in the room by the people closest to the work.

Before you can improve, train, or standardise a process, everyone needs to agree on how it actually works today. That is what a Discover session produces.

Sessions run at your site, around one table, with the people who do the work.

What You Get

What you walk out with

After the Session

The map is the start, not the deliverable

A wall of sticky notes is easy to produce and easy to forget. Every Discover session is written up into documentation your team can act on: the agreed process, the points where it breaks, and the priorities you set together.

We stay available to help you put it into practice, whether that means rewriting a procedure, redesigning a handover, or building the next step into how people are brought up to speed.

How It Works

Four moves,
one real process

A Discover session runs at your site, around one table, with the people who actually do the work.

01

Choose the process

Select a procedure, routine, handover, or workflow that matters. It can be one that is documented, one that has never been written down, or one that changes too often to keep up with.

02

Map the work

Physical cards on role lanes show who does what, when it happens, and how the work moves between people. The team builds it together, in the room, in their own words.

03

Add the context layer

Systems, tools, pains, risks, bottlenecks, waiting time, uncertainty, and opportunities get marked directly onto the map at the point where they occur.

04

Align and prioritise

Discuss the differences, agree on what is really happening, and decide what to improve, clarify, document, or explore further.
