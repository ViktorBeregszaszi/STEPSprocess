What is STEPS

A procedure training system your team can run on demand

STEPS is a self-facilitated physical board game built around your procedures. Your team plays through real scenarios, aligns on who does what and when, and understands the why behind the how.

It is not a PowerPoint presentation. It is not e-learning. It is a custom-built training tool designed around your actual procedures and verified with your own experts. It produces shared understanding across roles that documentation on its own cannot.

We build it with you. Your facilitators can run it indefinitely.

Sessions can be extended with a digital layer that captures where knowledge gaps recur across teams.

Example boards showing the format across different industries. Every client system is built from that client's own procedures.

Where STEPS Works Best

Built for industries where precision is non-negotiable

STEPS works best where the gap between what the procedure says and what people actually do carries real consequences.

High-stakes operations and costly mistakes Offshore energy, utilities, pharma, logistics, healthcare — anywhere a deviation from procedure has consequences beyond inconvenience.
Teams that work together physically Where roles are physically interdependent — one person's interpretation of a procedure directly shapes what the next person does.
Procedures that don't stick Technology, structure, resources — many things break procedures. STEPS focuses on the gap between what the process says and what each person believes it means.
